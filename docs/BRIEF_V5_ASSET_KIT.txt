PRESERVE THIS GAME
Public shared lobby, private solo/party rounds with up to 4 participants, ONE shared pile, exactly ONE hidden target geode each round. Players collect individual whole rocks into a basket, carry to their assigned processing table, deposit into its queue and crack them there. Finishing the target wins; pickup and deposit do not. Everyone retains their personally credited ordinary rubble. Winner collects one specimen for their lobby gallery. No new pets, eggs, PvP, trading, rebirths, durability, currencies or progression systems.

No cracking at the pile. No auto-harvesting the pile. No separate piles for each player. Machines process their owner's deposited queue while the owner collects. Keep permanent equipment ownership, currency, existing prices, rarity tables, individual specimen records, gallery ownership, party functionality and save compatibility. Do not reset player progress for an art update.
