## Concerns investigated (no change)
- **Hammer visible with Hand selected**: intended. `Hud.luau` tool slots are display only ("tools follow the context"); the held tool is stowed / shown by `CarryView` + `ToolAnim`. Its exact first-person position needs a Studio look (unverified).
- **Pickup / crack feedback**: action button names the state (MOVE CLOSER, BASKET FULL, HOLD TO CRACK, QUEUE full toasts), plate progress bar from the server, holds stop on menus. Unchanged.
- **Server authority**: inventory, purchases, payouts, rewards and the finish are server-side (`RockService`, `EconomyService`, `MatchService.finishRound` once per round, `AwardLedger`). The cheat fixes (sell scan, speed, sight fail-open, practice payouts) were done in another thread and are on main.
- **"Studio test: saves off"**: intentional Studio-only badge (`Hud.luau`, shown only when `RunService:IsStudio()` and saves are off). Not changed.
