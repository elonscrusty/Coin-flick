1 — CORE RULES / IMMUTABLE
A public SHARED LOBBY contains equipment showroom, party departure terminal, personal collection alcoves and social paths.
A party plays privately: 1-4 players, one central pile per match, exactly ONE hidden geode in that pile.
Only the party members participate. No unrelated strangers join a hunt. Everyone cracks the SAME pile and competes to be the first to uncover the geode.
The geode belongs to the player whose server-authorized action finishes cracking its rock. This includes their machine's action.
Other players retain rubble/cash earned from their own processing. No consolation geode. Winner gets specimen and multiplayer Win.
Solo hunts are supported for collection progression; record Solo Finds separately and do not inflate competitive Wins.
No player plots with private piles, no public shared search server, no combat/theft, account XP, compulsory rebirth, pets or eggs.
Equipment progression persists: Hammer -> Steel Hammer -> Jackhammer -> Automatic Cracker -> Hydraulic Press -> Industrial Crusher.
One installed machine per player per match plus one handheld; owned machines do not stack.
Higher equipment gives an earned competitive advantage. Explain each member's loadout before readying; no paid damage, speed, cash, luck or target hints at launch.

3 — ARENA GEOMETRY AND COMPETITION
One approximately 90 x 90 stud quarry court. Central mound footprint roughly 30 x 30; 12 stud work ring, four equally spaced machine bays around ring, one shared sell hopper or identical sell points on four sides.
Spawn each player at equivalent distance facing pile. Rotate positions between rematches.
Machines pull only exposed unclaimed rocks from central pile. No private sub-piles or duplicate rock reserves.
Rock damage is shared. Final valid damaging action owns ordinary rock rubble and target discovery. State this 'Finish cracking a rock to collect it.'
Target rock is assigned before round; identical shell to ordinary rocks. No visual tells, target attributes or special replicated model names.
Manual input targets currently exposed rocks within 14 studs and unobstructed line of sight. No mining through the mound.
A server queue handles actions ordered by eligible timestamp then receipt sequence; clients cannot choose timestamps for arbitration. Atomic HP/consumption means no double award.
For a machine batch, mark chosen exposed rocks as machine-owned processing reservations for at most 3 sec; reserved rocks get subtle moving-outline cue and cannot also be hit manually. Claimed rocks removed in authoritative processing order. Release expired claim if machine pauses/disconnects. Never reserve whole pile.
Players can target any other exposed rock. Reservations are visible, no invisible lock frustration.
When press batch contains target, owner wins when that target reaches processing completion, not on selection. Stop all remaining unprocessed jobs at match end; no reward for cancelled jobs.
Stop accepting all hits when target completes, even during victory visual delay.
No player body blocking: party avatars don't collide with one another. Machinery safely placed out of movement ring.
No PvP interference, shove or rock theft mechanic.
All rocks eventually reachable through deterministic settling/compaction; no inaccessible target beneath floor.
