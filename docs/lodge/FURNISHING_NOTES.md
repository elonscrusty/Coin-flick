# Prospector Lodge furnishings: notes

Furnishings for the new lobby (MASTER_PROMPT_Claude_Blender.md sections 7-14): Upgrades and Collection stalls,
the three-bay START HUNT assembly, two leaderboard frames, the RUBBLE SELL counter and the decor inventory
P01-P17, plus the three Collection display geodes and removable crystal clusters. The structural
Lodge_Building (floor, walls, roof, windows, doors, porch) is a separate kit and is not modelled here.

Status: built by Blender Python, exported as FBX, previewed offline and placed in an assembled preview scene.
Uploads and Studio checks are listed in "Verification" at the end. NOT verified in Studio.

## Axes, origins and the room conversion

Three frames are involved; mixing them up turns everything 180 degrees.

1. **Blender model space** (builders, this doc's sizes): 1 unit = 1 stud, +Z up, the asset's FRONT faces -Y.
   Origin = bottom centre, except wall pieces, which use the **centre of the back face** (the face that touches
   the wall or the sign): Lodge_WallLantern, Lodge_PosterGeode, Lodge_PosterMineral, Lodge_HammerEmblem.
   Lodge_Chandelier's origin is the bottom centre of the hanging lanterns (chains rise 8 above it).
2. **Roblox model space** (MeshCatalog Offsets / Attach, the uploaded Model's `Root`): the kit export maps
   Blender (x, y, z) to Roblox (-x, z, y). Front = Roblox -Z, so `Enum.NormalId.Front` of every face piece is its
   readable side.
3. **Room space** (Placement_Specification.csv, MASTER section 5): Blender X right, Y toward the rear, Z up,
   root (0, 0, 0) = Lodge root = the Lodge_Building pivot (floor centre, finished floor Z = 0).

**Room -> Roblox uses this repo's pipeline mapping, Roblox (X, Y, Z) = Blender (-X, Z, Y)** (ctrkit.to_roblox),
the same mapping the meshes and Lodge_Building use (docs/lodge/BUILD_NOTES.md). The master prompt's
(X, Z, -Y) is the same layout turned 180 degrees about the vertical; it is NOT used, so building and furnishings
share one frame. In Roblox: up +Y, the entrance faces -Z, the hunt wall is at +Z, the Upgrades side (Blender -X)
is Roblox +X. A Blender Z rotation `rot` becomes a yaw of the same angle about Roblox +Y (no extra turn):

```lua
-- lodgePivot = the CFrame Lodge_Building was pivoted to; X, Y, Z, yaw from the placement table below
kitModel:PivotTo(lodgePivot * CFrame.new(X, Y, Z) * CFrame.Angles(0, math.rad(yaw), 0))
```

Check: Upgrades_Stall (Blender -37, 0, 0, rot +90) -> Roblox (37, 0, 0), yaw 90: its counter (model -Z) turns to
Roblox -X, toward the room centre. Leaderboards (rot 0) keep facing -Z, toward the entrance.
Child decor placed on an attach point: `parentCFrame * CFrame.new(attach)` (attach values are in Roblox model
space, MeshCatalog `Attach`); the placement table already lists the resulting room positions.

inventory. Their crystal pieces (`Crystal1..3`, `Druse`) and `Lining` can be recoloured per mineral at runtime.

Collisions: render meshes should be `CanCollide = false`. Each colliding asset records simple boxes in the manifest
(`colliders`: size + offset in Roblox model space, listed below); build them as invisible anchored Parts. Rugs, pads,
lanterns, chandelier, posters, emblems, plants, trays and small decor have none (non-colliding). The hunt pads are
0.12 high: walkable, no collider needed.

## Decor placement choices

The CSV fixes the eleven assemblies; the decor positions (MASTER section 12 counts) are chosen here and are kept
out of the open centre (X -22..22, Y -20..14), the 8-stud access strips in front of the counters and boards, the
bay entrances and the leaderboard faces:
- Crates (6): pairs at the front ends of both stalls (Y about -14.5), one at the Upgrades rear end, one beside RUBBLE
  SELL (Y 22.6) with a lantern on top.
- Planters + plants (6): stall front ends (Y -18.4), rear corners (Y 31.8) and front corners (Y -31.8), all against the
  side walls (X +-39.6..40); none in front of a board or bay.
- Portable lanterns (8): Upgrades counter, Collection counter, RUBBLE SELL counter, the crate, four bay divider posts.
- Wall lanterns (6): four on the hunt frame's rear posts, two on the side walls at Y -17, Z 9 (between wall posts).
- Posters: high on the side walls above the stalls at Y -7, centre Z 15.75 (between the wall posts at Y -14 and 0 and
  below the side window sill Z 18.5; BUILD_NOTES gives Z 13.9..17.6 as free plaster, the 4-high poster overlaps the
  rails by about 0.15 at each edge, mounted proud of them). Geode poster on the Upgrades side, mineral poster on the
  Collection side, as in 00_Approved_Lobby.png.
- Upgrade runner: centred 8.2 in front of the Upgrades counter (Blender X -28.8), long side along the counter.
- Collection: three pedestals with the violet / blue / amber geodes on the counter, three trays with matching
  crystal clusters in the glazed lower display.
- Entry rug at the CSV root (0, -28, 0.025): its bottom then sits 0.025 above the floor (no z-fighting); use Z 0.005
  if it looks lifted.

## Differences from the references (known)

- The stalls are cantilevered (rear posts + canopy brackets) with no front posts, so the counters stay open; the
  awning is a broad low-pitch roof with one scallop per stripe (11 stripes), simpler than the sheet's deep rolled
  nose. The sign sits on the front fascia over the valance as in the sheet.
- Hunt bays: dividers are 3 high with a rail and a mid post; the rear frame (posts on the divider lines, beam at
  Z 9.6, knee braces) carries the sign at centre Z 11.5. With the arch and emblem the assembly reaches Z 14.6
  (spec "height 14"). The sheet's planters at the ends are separate Lodge_Planter/Plant instances (not placed there,
  to keep the bay sides clear).
- Leaderboard: 13.4 x 2.7 frame, 15.6 tall; the trophy on the header reaches 17.1 (spec 17). Board face 11.5 x 11.1
  (spec about 11.5 x 11.5) so the trophy stays at the 17 limit.
- Display geodes are 3.2 x 2.4 x 3.3 built from the sheet-12 geode kit (shell C, cathedral cluster); they read a bit
  more lumpy than the AssetSheet_03 icons.
- The 00 image is a generated illustration: its stalls/boards look closer and larger than the CSV positions give.
  The renders follow the CSV and the 05 plan (layout_plan.png matches it).
- Pegboard holes are a sparse grid of small dark squares (~40), not boolean holes or a texture.
