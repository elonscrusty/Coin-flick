## Coordinates (read this before placing anything)

- Authoring (Blender, = master prompt): 1 unit = 1 stud, X right, Y toward the rear (hunt wall), Z up.
  Origin (0, 0, 0) = finished interior floor top at the room centre. Clear interior X -42..42, Y -34..34.
- Export (this repo's pipeline, `ctrkit.to_roblox`, same as every kit mesh): **Roblox = (-X, Z, Y)**.
  So in Roblox: up is +Y, the front door faces **-Z**, the hunt wall is at **+Z**, and Blender -X (the
  Upgrades side in the master prompt) is Roblox **+X**.
- The master prompt suggests Roblox = (X, Z, -Y). That is the same layout turned 180 degrees about the
  vertical axis. Either is fine as long as the building and the furnishings use the SAME mapping. If the
  furnishings come through this pipeline too (`blender/build.py`), they share the mapping automatically:
  a furnishing at master-prompt Blender (x, y, z) goes at `lodgePivot * CFrame.new(-x, z, y)`, and a
  Blender Z rotation r becomes a Roblox Y rotation of r (front -Y -> -Z stays consistent).
- To place the whole building: clone `Kit.Lodge_Building`, `:PivotTo(cf)` where `cf` is the wanted
  floor-centre CFrame. Outside ground is at local Y -3 (Roblox), so the porch steps land on ground at
  `cf.Y - 3`. Plaque: clone `Kit.Lodge_Plaque` and `:PivotTo(cf * CFrame.new(Plaque attach))` (its
  origin is the back centre; it already faces -Z like the door).

## Renders

`Reverse`, `Cutaway` (roof hidden by render visibility only), `Exterior`, `Exterior_Front`; `*_blockout.png`

Comparison with 00_Approved_Lobby.png (`compare_Approved_Front.png`): same camera, room proportions,
side-wall stall zones, rear wall with three low bay windows behind the hunt bays and a high window band, high
side windows, exposed trusses and boarded ceiling. Differences: the spec roof (rise 14 over 43) is flatter
than the painting, so the trusses sit lower in frame and the rear gable glazing is mostly hidden behind the
tie beams; the painted rear wall shows larger single panes; the floor stones are bigger and flatter than the
painted cobbles (triangle budget). Exterior (`compare_Exterior.png`): timber frame, cream plaster, stone base,
corner posts, porch with three steps and rails, plaque above the door; the painted secondary gable and the gem
logo are left out on purpose (master prompt section 20).

## Limitations / open

- Not checked in Studio: import scale, the FBX axis handling and MeshPart sizes (largest piece ~92 x 15 x 76
  studs) are as for the other kit meshes, but this is the first model this large.
- No textures: the floor is ~600 flat flagstone meshes in two tones over a darker grout slab; plaster, wood and
  roof rely on colour + Roblox material.
- Colliders are box data in the manifest; nothing builds them yet. Gable / roof colliders are rough (players
  cannot reach them). The ramp needs a rotated invisible Part (`ramp` entry).
- Door leaves are fixed open; pieces carry `Anim = "Swing"` and a hinge `Pivot` if they ever need to move.
- The roof pitch follows the spec numbers (eaves 26, ridge 40 over a 86.4 span), so it is flatter than the
  painted references.
- Lights, lanterns, chandelier, plants, signs and all furnishings are not part of this asset.
