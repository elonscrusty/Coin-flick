# UI style audit: Roblox pet game, 17 shots

**Reference style:** white dotted panel, blue outline frame, red X, chunky outlined title, green/blue pill buttons, rounded corners.

**Caveat:** each cell is roughly 812×250 and the label strip covers the bottom edge. Some "clipped at the bottom" items may be a short capture viewport rather than a real bug. They are still worth checking at the real minimum screen size. I'm reading this from one downscaled sheet, so the small text is hard to read.

- **Coin counter:** it is a dark/black pill, not blue or white.

## Cross-cutting issues
1. **Panel size inconsistency:** the Pet Book window changes width between Pets (288px) and the other tabs (389px). Upgrades, Daily, Battle, Tower and Settings are all different widths. Fix one standard width per window class.
2. **Tiny text:** many subtitles, prices and footers are about 5–7px. Set a minimum of about 10–11px on a 1080p scale, or move them to a larger scale.
3. **Clipped content:** nearly every tall panel is cut at the bottom. Check the viewport and panel height constraints, and make the content scroll with a padded footer.
4. **Off-palette buttons:**
   - dark navy: NO PETS YET, EQUIP BEST;
   - gold: OPEN, EQUIPPED;
   - black OFF toggles;
   - green SOON pills that are disabled.
   - Standardise on the green primary / blue secondary / grey disabled set.
5. **Coin pill:** it is dark on the HUD, Upgrades, Daily and Shop. Pick one style.
6. **Tabs:** Pet Book and Upgrades tabs sit in a white bar. Shop tabs sit in a dark bar. Unify them.
7. **Empty states:** Pet Book has a CTA. Battle and Tower have no CTA and show active buttons. Pet Book Display is only a disabled bar.
8. **Stray cursors:** these hide tab labels in the shop and Pet Book. Exclude the cursor from captures, or confirm that no real UI element is a hand-cursor sprite.
9. **FREE PET! banner:** it sits right above the panel in u_01–u_11. Check that it never overlaps the red X on small screens.

## Restyle priority (highest first)
1. **Shop, all four tabs (u_12–u_15).** It is the biggest gap. It has no panel or frame, the palette is wild, the text is tiny, and it is the money screen. Rebuild it inside the standard white dotted, blue-frame window with a shared card style. Fix the empty Passes space and the Potions emptiness.
2. **HUD (u_00).** It is always on screen. Unify the left buttons and the coin pill, and fix the muted colours and tiny subtitles. Make sure the dev panel never opens on its own, and hide DEV in production.
3. **Daily (u_09).** Replace the gold OPEN with a green pill. Fix the flat fonts, clipped quests, overlapping "MORE QUESTS", and tiny day tiles.
4. **Upgrades (u_06).** Switch to the white dotted panel. Fix the muddy gold EQUIPPED button and the ambiguous Gear/Robux tabs. Fix the clipped list and detail pane, and the truncated copy.
5. **Pet Book Display (u_05) and Stats (u_04).** Fix the navy bar and the gold selection. Match the Stats row style to Records, and lift the tiny text. Standardise the window width across the five tabs, which also affects u_01.
6. **Tower (u_11).** Make EQUIP BEST a blue pill. Match the Battle width and add an empty-state CTA and disabled buttons. Apply the same fixes to Battle (u_10).
7. **Settings (u_08).** Swap the black OFF toggles and fix the clipped footer.
8. **Pet Book Book tab (u_02).** Raise the tiny text and make sure the Meadow list is visible.
9. **DEV panel (u_16).** Gate it behind an admin check. Restyle only if players can ever see it.

Start Hunt (u_07), Pet Book Pets and Records (u_01, u_03), and Battle (u_10) are your reference screens. Copy their frame, title, button and empty-state patterns into the others.
